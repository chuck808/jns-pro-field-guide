    Quick Start

    From power-on to a recorded run in six steps.

    1
    Power on

    Turn on the controller and the devices supplied with your kit.

    2
    Open Gate Pro

    Use the ANO control to highlight Gate Pro and press to select.

    3
    Reset

    Arm the run and get into your starting position.

    4
    Start

    Begin the start sequence when you are ready.

    5
    Ride

    React to the sequence and ride your run normally.

    6
    Review

    Check your result, then continue or finish the session.

    ●
    ANO controlRotate = move · Press = select/edit · Hold only where the screen tells you to
